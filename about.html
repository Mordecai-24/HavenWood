About HavenWood

A Nairobi furniture business built on one idea: a better space makes for a happier life.

Our story

HavenWood began with a simple frustration: it was hard to find furniture that was both strong and fairly priced. Pieces were either flimsy and cheap, or well made and out of reach.

So we set out to make furniture the way it should be made. We choose sturdy timber, keep the designs clean and comfortable, and build each piece to be used every day for many years.

Our name says it all. A haven is a place where you feel safe and at ease, and wood is the material we trust to build it.

Our mission

To make strong, comfortable and fairly priced furniture that helps families and businesses create spaces they are proud of.

Our vision

To be the furniture brand Kenyans trust first when they want something well made, and to build better spaces and happier lives across East Africa.

What we value

Honest materials

We use real hardwood and quality fabric, and we tell you exactly what each piece is made from.

Fair prices

Good furniture should be within reach. We keep our designs simple so the money goes into the materials.

Careful craftsmanship

Every piece is checked before it leaves the workshop, because small details decide how long furniture lasts.

Service after the sale

We deliver, set up and stand behind what we sell. If something is wrong, we put it right.
